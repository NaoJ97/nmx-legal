NMX Cash Engine

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Applies to the NMX Cash Engine application

NMX Cash Engine is a private, single-user tool built for DJ NaoMiX (operated under NMX Productions) to manage and publish his own original music content on his own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not a public product and is not used by, or intended for, anyone other than the account owner.

01

Who operates this tool

NMX Cash Engine is operated by NMX Productions (DJ NaoMiX), an independent music production and label entity based in France. Contact details are at the end of this page.

02

What data we access

The tool connects, via official OAuth authorization, only to accounts belonging to the operator (the TikTok and YouTube accounts of DJ NaoMiX). Through TikTok's Content Posting API, it requests the video.upload scope only — the ability to upload a video as a private draft to the authenticated user's own TikTok inbox.

  • No content is posted publicly or automatically through the API.
  • No data belonging to any other TikTok or YouTube user is accessed, read, or collected.
  • No advertising identifiers, trackers, or analytics SDKs are used.
Every video still requires a manual review and publish step by the account owner inside the TikTok app before it becomes visible to anyone.
03

How data is used

Authorization tokens are used exclusively to upload the operator's own video files as drafts, and to read basic account/channel statistics (subscriber count, view count) for the operator's own reporting. Nothing is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.

04

Storage & retention

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device and are never transmitted anywhere beyond the official TikTok and YouTube APIs. Tokens remain valid until the operator revokes access, which can be done at any time from the TikTok or Google account security settings.

05

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the tool evolves. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.
